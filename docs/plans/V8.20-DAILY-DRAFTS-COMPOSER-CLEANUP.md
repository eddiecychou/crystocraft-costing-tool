## QA lesson

Use replacement fill for existing login fields. Native typeText appends to
retained/autofilled text: one production attempt was stopped by browser email
validation before authentication. Replacing both fields explicitly then signing
in passed. That input mistake was not an app defect and required no source,
credential or permission change. Source recovery and DOM/geometry parity are
stronger extraction evidence than claiming all screenshots are pixel-identical;
record the one observed screenshot difference and its location honestly.
