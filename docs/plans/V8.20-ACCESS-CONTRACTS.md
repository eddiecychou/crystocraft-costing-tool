## Findings and next boundary

Human role/module decisions agree for these paths. Daily Drafts AI and draft
assets require marketing; CRM lead records/files require customers. Reusing a
helper must preserve that distinction. Endpoint method/AI-key checks occur
before shared-style authentication. Non-OK profile responses deny access;
transport/JSON exceptions currently propagate and are explicitly characterized.
Service identities, account approval/suspension and other endpoints are outside
this pilot; see the matrix for the exact limits.

Next proposed slice: migrate only draft-outreach-topic's duplicate gate to
requireModule(req, 'marketing'), preserving method/config checks, prompts,
payloads and responses. This batch does not authorize that migration. No real
customer emails, AI requests, live fixtures, account edits, stock/finance writes
or Firebase rule releases occurred. Production should remain the prior verified
bulk-toolbar deployment, 6ae886d9008d59024fdead2f.
