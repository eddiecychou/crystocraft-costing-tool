# New Quote exchange-rate release

A Settings response can fill untouched fields on New Quote. A staff-entered
or deliberately cleared rate takes precedence. Missing or invalid values
require manual entry or explicit acceptance of the suggestions. The form
saves finite positive rates at their entered precision. The same Settings
reader supplies the Corporate Gifts pricing guard.
