The Sales Invoices route and staff print route require `invoices`. Their Firestore order and line reads require `shipping` for staff. `useOrders` ends loading after a denied read and leaves the list empty. That makes lack of access look like a confirmed empty result. The JES history API requires `erp`, and existing ledger reads and Re-sync use the `uc` gateway. With no visible OC invoice numbers, the page's financial check returns an empty result locally without reaching that gateway. [Route gates](../../src/App.jsx#L224) · [Sales Invoices list](../../src/pages/SalesInvoices.jsx#L75) · [Order listener](../../src/shipping.js#L188) · [Rules](../../firestore.rules#L1235) · [ERP gateway](../../netlify/edge-functions/erp.js#L150) · [Ledger gateway](../../netlify/edge-functions/uc.js#L45)

The customer list filters out drafts and requires an invoice identifier. The direct customer document currently checks ownership, then loads the lines and takes the `erp_so_no` fallback even when it contains a sales order number. Owner access was enforced in this test. The reproduced inconsistency concerns which of the owner's records become printable invoices. ## Proposed fixes in order

1. **Align the customer invoice document with the portal list.** Share the app-invoice eligibility check between the two views. For an owned record, require an eligible non-draft status and resolve an actual SI with the existing `orderSi` helper before loading lines or offering PDF or Excel. Preserve confirmed legacy invoices whose SI was stored in the SO field. Use a short unavailable message for an ineligible document. Test issued, draft with an SI, uninvoiced with an SO, legacy SI, and another customer's invoice with fictional records.
2. **Make staff access limits and partial exports visible.** In Sales Invoices, show distinct states for an unavailable OC source, an unavailable JES source and a successful search with zero results. Surface unexpected order-read failures with Retry. Only offer a combined CSV after both sources load, and permit a clearly labelled export of an explicitly selected available source. Explain the missing Shipping access on a staff invoice deep link. Show the financial check and Re-sync only with their existing capabilities. Keep the exact-SI check for historical links and the separate current-order controls. Test the roles in the table, both source failures, explicit one-source export and combined export.
3. **Confirm what invoice-only staff need before granting access.** The proposed default for this role is a read-only issued-invoice list, exact invoice detail and print/Excel. The [invoice-only staff review](V8.20-INVOICE-ONLY-STAFF-REVIEW.md) documents fictional source tests, the minimum fields and the missing-original workflow for approval before implementation.

## Staff source availability and CSV scope released

16 November 2026 AEDT. Sales Invoices uses the existing Shipping and ERP capabilities to show which source can load. A failed order or archive read shows an unavailable state and Retry. A cache-only OC snapshot waits for the server before its records are eligible for export. Selecting a source and date range filters the invoice table and CSV together. The combined scope requires both sources to load. Staff can explicitly select either available source when the other is unavailable.

The export button and filename identify the sources represented in the CSV, and the original row-level Source column continues to identify App or JES. OC retains precedence when a row also appears in JES. WooCommerce accounting has its own OC-labelled download. The staff print route explains when Shipping access is required. The financial check and Re-sync require the existing Shipping and UC permissions.

Twelve fictional emulator cases exercised actual route gates, Firestore rules, and the ERP and UC handlers. The fixtures covered invoice-only access, each source separately, both sources, an archive failure and Retry, staff print, and customer boundaries.
