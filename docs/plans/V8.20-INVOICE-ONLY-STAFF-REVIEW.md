## Minimum proposed access

- **Capability.** After approval, authenticate staff at a dedicated read-only invoice endpoint and check their existing Invoices capability on every list, detail or download request. Resolve the invoice by exact source and SI. Staff with the Invoices module get the agreed company scope of issued invoices. Admin and current combined roles retain their existing routes. The archived JES source keeps its ERP boundary unless an invoice-specific JES scope is separately approved.
- **List and CSV.** Return only SI, invoice date, customer display name, currency, issued or void status, invoice total when known, source, and document availability. Filters and CSV use the same scope. Keep unknown amounts blank. A void entry may appear as history with its status explicit. Use separate invoice identities for splits and reissues even when they share a UC or order.
- **Exact document and Excel.** Return an original or a verified, immutable as-issued document view for the selected SI. An approved structured view needs the fields appearing on the issued document, such as SI, date, currency, UC/PO references used on the invoice, bill-to details, item descriptions, quantities, units where recorded, selling amounts, and the applicable discount, freight, tax, deposits and total. Use customer-facing remarks only. Any historical Excel output needs the original workbook or verified structured invoice data.
- **Data boundary.** Serve an explicit field list and authorize each private file download. Exclude editable order records, private allocation receipts, CRM files, internal remarks, cost and margin, stock, Packing and Freight records, and bank or settings collections. Invoice issuance, split/reissue, edits, UC operations, financial Re-sync and ERP browsing continue to use their current authorization. Keep the customer-facing documents on their current scoped routes.

## Missing originals and release order

Show **Original unavailable** next to the known SI and retain its verified summary. The detail should say **Original invoice [SI] is unavailable. Ask Finance for the issued copy.** Keep Print and Excel unavailable for that document. Use a separate retryable state for a temporary source failure. A conflicting SI, amount, currency or ownership reference stays **Needs review** until Finance verifies the evidence.

Finance can supply the issued file through an approved internal archive process. Before registering an exact link, verify the SI, issuer, customer, issue date, currency and total against the record and preserve the original bytes and provenance. A verified PDF can be viewed or downloaded by the role. Excel requires a verified workbook or structured snapshot. The archive can preserve an explicitly voided copy for reference with its status visible. An older SI never inherits the current order's lines, even when the header and total agree. Historic records without an original stay visible as summaries.

If approved, deliver the read-only OC list and exact-SI status first. Add original-file viewing and structured exports when the authority is available. The proposed role and fields should be confirmed before any endpoint or permission changes.
