# Void PI and Void SI

## Operator workflow

Open the saved PI from the Shipping order and choose **Void PI**. The confirmation identifies the order document number and PI revision. Enter a reason and confirm. Reopening the voided revision shows its original reference and void history. **+ New Rev** on the order creates a separate PI revision that can be printed once saved.

In **Sales Invoices**, expand **Void sales invoice**, enter the complete SI number, and choose **Find invoice**. Confirm the number, customer, date, UC and total before choosing **Void SI** and entering a reason. The action is available to admins and staff with Sales Invoices and UC Registry permissions. It accepts an issued OC ledger row, including a split or reissued SI. JES-only invoices stay in the read-only JES archive.

The reason, actor and time are recorded. The prior document numbers stay reserved. Already-downloaded files remain copies as issued. The void action performs no refund, credit note, order closure, stock movement or customer notification.

## Record handling

PI history lives under `orders/{orderId}/closure_audit` and is keyed by the saved document number and PI revision. The server compares the reviewed header version and uses a Firestore transaction to preserve one entry per revision. Existing rules protect history from direct client writes. The order deletion service checks for history before deleting an unused draft.

SI identity comes from the exact ledger ID and SI number, with an optimistic timestamp check. `erp-sync/migrations/20261127000000_document_void.sql` adds nullable void metadata, makes the ledger row read-only after void, and extends the existing ledger audit trigger to record status changes. The service-only transaction fences late financial follow-ups for the exact receipt. Other invoices, their receipts and the shared UC keep their saved values. The buyer portal excludes the voided SI from OC history and any JES mirror with the same number. The audit reason stays internal.
