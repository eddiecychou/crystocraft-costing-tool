## Ownership and preserved behavior

`components/shipping/ShipmentPageHeader.jsx` owns the existing title/help/back
link, proforma/invoice links, Raise Invoice control and allocation feedback, plus
Order & Lines / Packing List / Freight tab markup. It is controlled by parent
props and renders a Fragment, preserving the existing DOM, classes, labels,
ordinary anchors, new-tab targets/rel values, title precedence and busy/error
states. It has no state, effects, business reads, writes or allocation authority.

ShipmentForm owns route identity, reads/Retry, all state, doAllocateSi and number
persistence, saves, errors/pending status and navigation. It passes callbacks to
the header and still mounts PackingListEditor/FreightComparison itself. All
creation, quote conversion, money calculations, source/line identity, stock,
financial synchronization and export workflows remain in their existing owners.
The page is 63 lines shorter (962 → 899); the extracted component has 84 lines
including its imports, controlled interface and ownership comment.
