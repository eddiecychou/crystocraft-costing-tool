# Crystocraft Range buyer catalogue release

Verified on 19 November 2026 at 16:48 AEDT. Application commit `70876f2ed5ad100bdaf9045765f120febebb7688`.

## Buyer access

The catalogue endpoint checks that the signed-in buyer is approved. It returns published Range designs and the buyer’s selling price for each finish. Product costing, plating costs, suppliers and manufacturing markup stay in staff records. The same publication check covers direct links, featured images, availability, proposals, favourites and saved Range enquiry lines.

Historical designs without a visibility flag retain their published status. Hiding a design or marking it retired and sold out withdraws it. Visible retired stock can still receive enquiries. This release required a Firestore rule update and no product or price migration.
