# Legacy Shipping navigation and Direct Invoice visibility

The consolidated Production page owns all three tabs. `/shipments` opens `/shipping` on Orders. `/logistics` opens `/shipping?tab=logistics`, including the vendor form's **← Logistics** link and the return after vendor deletion. Vendor detail and creation, order detail and creation, PI, invoice and Packing List URLs retain their existing routes. Tab selection is stored in the URL and follows browser history and refresh. The unused standalone Shipments and Logistics list modules were removed.

Sales Invoices shows **Direct Invoice** only when the current role has access to Shipping. The creation URL's existing Shipping gate still applies. The release changes navigation and visibility. It performs no data migration or rules update.

## Verification

Nine offline checks used the real router, Shipping, vendor form and Sales Invoices with fictional orders, invoices, vendors and profiles. They covered old list aliases, vendor detail and creation returns, a fictional deletion, tab browser history, saved-order navigation, document routes, ERP archive details, and role-specific visibility. Admin and staff with both modules could open the Direct Invoice route. Staff with Invoices alone kept Sales Invoices and lacked the Shipping action. Approved customers stayed within their own portal.
