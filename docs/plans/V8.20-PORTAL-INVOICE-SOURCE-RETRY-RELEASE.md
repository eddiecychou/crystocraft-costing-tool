# Portal invoice history during an outage

In **My Orders → Sales Invoice History**, OC and JES load independently. A buyer can open invoices as soon as either source returns. A failed or timed-out source shows its name and a Retry button. Retrying requests only that source and preserves the other invoices, their links and the number of rows already shown. When the source recovers, the existing invoice merge restores the full date order and resolves duplicate invoice numbers.

The empty-history and shared-account messages require two successful responses. Invalid lists trigger a retry notice. Changing buyer or linked company clears the view and cancels pending requests. Invoice ownership, issued status, split and reissued invoice identities, original-copy links and export logic use the existing customer endpoints. This was a frontend release with no data migration.

## Fictional verification

- Two fictional buyers signed in to the Auth and Firestore emulators and opened the real My Orders page in Chrome at a mobile viewport. With OC unavailable, one buyer saw their JES invoice. With JES unavailable, the other buyer saw their OC invoice. Each retry restored that buyer's two invoices. Request counts confirmed a single call to the healthy source and two to the retried source.
- The CI tests rendered the portal page with the actual HTTP client and controlled responses. They covered a slow source, either source failing, both failing, a second failure, invalid data, a timeout, and switching buyers with a retry in flight. The healthy invoice remained visible and a late response could not populate the new buyer's list.
- Invoice identity, merge and privacy regressions covered first, split, reissued and legacy records. Local build, undefined-name lint and Firebase configuration tests passed.
