# Add items after reservation — V8.20, 29 October 2026

Released 29 October 2026 after owner authorization, both exact-SHA GitHub checks,
and fresh admin/customer sign-ins on candidate and production. No live order or
inventory record was edited during release verification.

## Cause and resulting behavior

`OrderInventoryIssue.jsx` renders Crystal and Packaging stock with two branches.
Its open branch has the item picker and Add line control; its reserved branch
only had existing quantity edits, production-in and release. Consequently, the
ability to add stock disappeared immediately after Reserve. Metal components
already had an add-to-reservation control in `OrderStockIssue.jsx`.

The reserved Crystal and Packaging panels now offer **Add to reservation**:
select an additional item, enter a whole quantity, review availability, and confirm
**Reserve item**. Already reserved items are excluded from the picker; their
existing quantity editor remains available. Cancellation leaves the draft and
stock unchanged. Failure retains the entered item/quantity and shows an alert.
Production-in still locks additions. No release/re-reserve is required.

The shared `addReservedLine` now appends the new order row, writes the reserve
movement and updates its cached balance in one Firestore transaction. Reads of
the order serialize concurrent additions; duplicate targets are refused. The new
item's on-hand quantity and every existing reservation line are preserved.
Stock code, stage, legacy-issued state, quantity and history checks run again
inside the transaction. An old `add_*` movement without its saved order row
requires review rather than silently reserving twice.

Per-class optional order counters `component_lines_add_sequence`,
`crystal_lines_add_sequence`, and `packaging_lines_add_sequence` give new
`add_v2_{orderId}_g{generation}_{itemId}_a{sequence}` movements distinct IDs.
No old movement, reservation timestamp, generation, BOM-gap or commercial field
is rewritten. Rules and permissions are unchanged. Existing full reserve,
quantity-edit, remove, release and production-in implementations otherwise remain
unchanged; this is not a rewrite of the entire reservation lifecycle.
