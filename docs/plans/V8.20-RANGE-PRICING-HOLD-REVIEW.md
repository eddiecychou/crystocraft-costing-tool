# Range pricing hold review and proposed change

26 November 2026, Australia/Sydney. This review used the released source
`c27ad39e` and fictional records. The B0016 setup record establishes its
historical starting state. Current production product data was outside the
fixture review. [B0016 setup record](B0016-LIVE-SETUP-COMPLETE-2026-10-15.md)

## Findings

- **B0016 is the only design covered by the Range Costing hold.** The page
  matches design number `0016` and format `000`. It hides the finished-product
  estimates, shows the assembly and packaging reason, and blocks **Save &
  publish**. **Save costing** still writes a draft. The hold comes from the
  loaded product and a hardcoded condition. A different design has no
  configurable hold. [Hold and estimates](../../src/pages/RangeCosting.jsx#L305)
- **Costing publication and buyer prices are independent.** Save & publish
  writes the `costing` map with `published_at` and `markup_used`. The normal
  Range product editor writes `variants[].ws_price_usd` and visibility via
  the family transaction. The catalogue API uses that saved variant price
  and the buyer's price factor. Range price-list exports and quote selection
  also consume saved prices. A last-published costing timestamp gives no
  evidence that a selling price changed.
  [Costing save](../../src/pages/RangeCosting.jsx#L359) ·
  [Range form](../../src/pages/RangeForm.jsx#L971) ·
  [Buyer projection](../../netlify/functions/lib/customerRange.js#L9) ·
  [Price-list export](../../src/rangeOrderForm.js#L76) ·
  [Staff quote selection](../../src/rangeQuote.js#L49)
- **Other paths have their own purpose.** Quick entry and ERP import start
  hidden concepts. The approved Range uploader preserves existing variant
  prices and initializes new variant prices to null. The legacy catalogue
  seed can import wholesale prices on new rows. B0016's dedicated setup had
  exact scope, a hidden concept and six null prices. Its component-link
  operation updates the approved component references. Preserve those
  paths and the existing service permission boundaries.
  [Quick entry](../../src/rangeQuickEntry.js#L3) ·
  [ERP import](../../src/erpProductImport.js#L188) ·
  [Range uploader](../../src/domain/rangeUpload.js#L107) ·
  [Catalogue seed](../../src/pages/Range.jsx#L97) ·
  [B0016 rules](../../firestore.rules#L112)
- **A draft save can clear the costing publication marker.** A fictional
  previously published record lost `published_at` and `markup_used` after
  **Save costing** replaced the serialized map. Its variant price remained
  available. A hold needs its own state, independent of the publication
  marker. [Costing serialization](../../src/pages/RangeCosting.jsx#L183) ·
  [Costing save](../../src/pages/RangeCosting.jsx#L359)

## Proposed first change

1. **Store an internal costing hold per Range family.** Use an optional
   `pricing_hold` field on the existing `range_products/{id}` document with
   active state, a short reason, and the last change time and actor. Keep it
   outside the editable `costing` map. Show the reason on Range Costing and
   allow an explicit set or clear action for existing authorized costing
   operators. Reuse the Pricing route and verify its permissions against
   the Figurine write rule. Restrict changes to the hold field to the same
   authorized operators while preserving ordinary product edit permissions.
2. **Keep B0016 protected during rollout.** Read the stored hold and retain
   the current design/format guard as a fallback during migration. Verify
   the exact existing B0016 family and its current state by readback before
   an approved data write. Seed the hold in place with the existing reason.
   Retire the fallback only after verifying the replacement protection at
   the normal entry point. A legacy record without a hold keeps its usual
   workflow.
3. **Check the current hold at save time.** Keep component input, draft save
   and the existing missing-cost checks available. Hide the finished price
   estimates while the hold is active. Check the latest hold and conflicting
   costing edits in the publish transaction. A tab opened before a hold
   should keep its draft and show the reason when publish is denied.
   Keep set/clear as a targeted write so another editor cannot clear a hold
   while saving unrelated catalogue fields.
4. **Preserve approved buyer prices and visibility.** The first change
   applies to costing publication. Previously approved prices continue to
   come from the variant and buyer factor. B0016 retains its existing
   protection during migration. Explicitly freezing changes to an existing
   variant selling price would need a separate decision, since the normal
   Range editor and the catalogue seed control those prices independently
   of costing. Keep the internal reason out of customer projections and
   catalogue exports.

Before implementation or migration, exercise legacy B0016, a stored hold on
a different family, an ordinary legacy record, a stale two-tab publish, and
a deliberate clear. Confirm that complete costing can publish after release,
incomplete costing still stops, unrelated saves keep the hold, existing
prices continue to resolve, and the buyer payload and exports omit internal
hold details. Check the ordinary Range editor, importer and scoped MCP
contracts with fictional records.

## Local implementation

- Authorized Range Costing staff can set a hold with a reason or clear it.
  Each action updates only `pricing_hold` with the signed-in actor and a
  server timestamp. The B0016 design/format protection takes precedence
  and its existing reason remains visible. This slice uses the fallback
  for B0016 in both the application and Firestore rules.
- **Save & publish** reads the current product in a Firestore transaction.
  An active hold blocks the write and returns its reason to the editor.
  Concurrent edits to the stored costing also block publication. The
  editor retains its draft. **Save costing** still writes just the draft.
  Firestore rules protect held costing from older tabs and limit hold
  edits to staff with both Figurine and Pricing access, or an admin.
- Fictional checks covered two Chrome tabs, draft save and explicit
  release of a hold, B0016 legacy protection, and unchanged catalogue
  access and prices. The fictional USD 12 wholesale price continued to
  resolve to USD 10.80 for a buyer at 90%. Existing buyer and staff exports
  use the same price fields. Local rules tests verified each role and
  preserved the scoped Range uploader workflows.

The application and Firestore rules are prepared locally. A release
requires the existing exact-commit checks and the Firestore rules gate.
Publishing the accompanying rules first protects earlier browser tabs.
