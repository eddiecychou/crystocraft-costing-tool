ShipmentForm supplies the destination fields, remarks, quantity visibility
and callbacks. The parent owns customer autofill, address merging, validation
and persistence. The existing print pages consume the saved remarks and
quantity preference.

edits, saving and reopening. Proforma and Sales Invoice print fixtures
cover multiline remarks and both quantity visibility settings.

the saved destination and remarks fields across refresh. Saved orders
are viewed only. Evidence is in qa/out/destination-remarks.
