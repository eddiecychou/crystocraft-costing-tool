# Dashboard audit and customer-workbench proposal — 10 November 2026

Status: implementation completed; commit/push/deploy approved by the owner on 10 November 2026.
Smoke tests and the mandatory exact-SHA/candidate/production gates are in progress.
No production business records, rules, emails or migration changed. Read-only audit
sign-in telemetry may be stamped. Dates in this report use Australia/Sydney.

## Owner's workflow

1. See and handle every outstanding customer task, including future/undated work.
2. See customers currently being dealt with and what is happening: exploration,
   quotations, ordering and production. Most relationships are recurring.
3. Review communication context when needed, starting recently and expanding to
   2, 3, 6 and 12 months. Summaries and longer text belong in a second layer.
4. Use the existing Daily Drafts workflow for customers not contacted for about
   three months. Do not create a competing outreach queue or send automatically.
5. Work can last months or half a year. A quiet communication period or a finished
   task must not silently end a project/customer relationship.
6. Creating a task should add/keep that customer in active work. A reference note
   should remain reference information, without starting work or resetting contact.

## Dashboard customer scope — owner clarification

The dashboard proposal is **B2B only**. Apply one shared scope to open/due/waiting
tasks and their counts, active customer work and stage rows, recent-contact review,
customer search/picker, summary/detail entry points and linked order/production
figures. Remove the Retail Customer toggle from this dashboard. Do not show an
all-customer count above a B2B-only list.

Use existing customer classifications, not names or email-domain guesses:

- Explicit `customer_type: retail` identifies B2C records, including retail sync
  records. Legacy exact `Retail Customer` tags are the retail fallback.
- Explicit/reviewed B2B classification stays included. A wholesale retail chain
  is B2B; the generic free tag `Retail` describes an industry and is not the
  `Retail Customer` flag. Flag conflicting/dual classifications for review;
  do not silently rewrite records to make the filter work.
- Preserve older B2B accounts without the newer `customer_type` field. Do not
  turn this into an empty-field exclusion or a Prospect/Active-only filter.
- Match linked order/production counts to the same scope; explicitly B2C orders
  and shared retail accounts must not leak into those figures. WooCommerce
  origin alone is not proof that a particular business buyer/order is B2C.
- Retail records, tasks, history and orders remain available in their existing
  operational areas. This is a dashboard view scope, not deletion, migration,
  account suspension or a global change to marketing audiences.
- The Daily Drafts handoff should use its existing B2B audience. Other audiences
  remain available within Marketing; do not create a second outreach queue.

The earlier measured live counts below are **before this proposed scope**; they
must not be presented as the post-change B2B counts.

## Proposed dashboard

### First layer: compact actions and customer rows

- **My tasks:** default **All open**, with explicit Due and Waiting filters.
  Keep overdue, today, upcoming and undated items visible in All open; label
  waiting clearly. Display task, customer, due/follow-up date and completion.
  Keep “Show all open” visible when narrowed. Remove summary-only/note-only cards.
- **Customers I'm dealing with:** one compact row per customer: name, current
  work stages, last actual contact and open-task count. Clicking the name opens
  details. Recurring customers can simultaneously show Quotation and Order & Production.
- **Review period:** 1, 2, 3, 6 months and 1 year for contacted-customer review.
  Ongoing work outside that window stays visible and is labelled accordingly.
  The period never filters tasks or closes work. Distinguish contact dates from
  task due dates, project dates, AI-cache age and the selected summary context.
- **Daily Drafts:** a short handoff into Marketing's existing Daily Drafts tab,
  not another composer/queue. Production/order totals remain compact links to
  the existing operational pages rather than four dominant colored tiles.
- One primary Add task control, rather than Add task/Add note repeated on every
  customer. Keep category/search controls compact and secondary.

### Second layer: customer detail

Open a customer panel/drawer for project/work records, full task text and history,
email/conversation summary, reference notes and the existing interaction history.
Default summary/notes closed. Preserve edit, complete/reopen and legacy follow-up
functions; do not hide full text behind irreversible truncation.

Tasks and work stay distinct:

- Add task: create once and add/keep the customer in active work, preserving
  existing work, revisions and review history. This is an explicit human action.
- Add note: save reference only; no task, active-work activation, relationship
  conversion, contact-date update, outreach enrolment or sending.
- Complete task: complete that action only. Other projects/quotes/orders remain.
- A customer remains a customer after a particular job completes. Do not use a
  lead-conversion funnel or silently rewrite Prospect/Active CRM status.
- Exploration, Quotation and Order & Production labels come from the relevant saved
  work/quote/order records or an explicit operator choice. Do not invent stages
  from AI prose or assign one highest stage that hides parallel work.

### Three business stages — owner clarification

Combine Ordering and Production into **Order & Production**. The main dashboard
has only these three business stages:

| Stage | Proposed source |
|---|---|
| Exploration | An explicitly confirmed current discussion about products, ideas, requirements or samples. Do not infer this just because a task or note was added. |
| Quotation | A linked open client quote in Draft or Sent; its detail panel distinguishes preparation from a sent quote. Confirmed/lost quotes are not open quotations. |
| Order & Production | A current confirmed order and its preparation/production/packing/ready-to-ship work. Existing Confirmed, Packing and Ready order statuses fit this combined group. Explicitly confirmed order preparation without a saved order can be operator-recorded. |

Use actual source records or an explicit operator choice. A historical confirmed
quote alone must not reactivate old fulfilled business. Resolve current linked
orders/work rather than blindly treating every Confirmed/Won quote as outstanding.
Shipped/delivered orders leave this active group; other ongoing projects/quotes
for that customer remain visible. Preserve detailed order statuses on the order.
This is a dashboard grouping, not a new production-start field or rewriting of
stored order/quote statuses. A new task activates customer work but leaves the
business stage unset until supported; “Stage not set” is a missing value, not a
fourth business stage.

### Long-running work and last-contact definition

Work remains open until explicitly finished, cancelled or paused, not until a
summary window or arbitrary inactivity limit expires. A six-month exploration
can coexist with a repeat order already in production. Closing one must not
close the other. Review/chase dates remind the operator without declaring loss.

For review and the three-month sweep, use the latest known actual customer
communication from synced email, WhatsApp and recorded calls/interactions,
including relevant outgoing/incoming activity. Draft creation, reference notes,
task saves and AI refreshes are not customer contact. Missing or incomplete
history stays “contact date unknown”; do not treat it as a verified 90-day gap.
Recorded outreach and contact date remain distinct fields/meanings.

## Implementation order proposed for approval

1. **Task list and second layer.** Correct the information hierarchy; show all
   open task states/dates, preserve filters and mobile access, move paragraphs
   off the main screen. Add the task-to-active-work bridge atomically with its
   stable create ID/retry behavior and existing revision/history safeguards.
   Repeated saves must not duplicate tasks or work positions; concurrent edits
   must preserve drafts and existing requests. Notes remain independent.
2. **Customer-work overview and review periods.** Reuse linked saved quotes and
   orders, add 60-day review support, keep ongoing older work visible, show
   multiple current stages and unknown dates honestly. Preserve all 20 existing
   Pipeline positions/history. Do not automatically split free text into new
   projects or bulk-map records using AI. Review any new project schema/migration
   separately before applying it; start with existing links and reviewed work.
   Use shared maps/projections rather than per-customer subscription fanout.
3. **Daily Drafts alignment.** Add an explicit existing-customer “quiet 90 days”
   targeting mode using the verified contact-date projection. Keep the existing
   14-day outreach, topic cooldown, blocking, deduplication and human-review/send
   behavior. Do not simply replace 14 with 90: these are different rules.
   Carry current work context so a project follow-up is not a generic new pitch.
   Reviewed-customer tracking can then avoid rereading the same customers when
   broadening a review window; new communication makes them reviewable again.

## Local implementation — 10 November 2026

- Dashboard now presents all open B2B tasks first, with Due and Waiting drilldowns.
  Compact customer rows show parallel work stages, recorded contact date and open
  task count. Review choices are 30/60/90/180/365 days; older ongoing work remains.
  Summaries, notes, full task editing, archived/completed items and work review
  remain available in the second layer. Existing AI update state survives navigation.
- New open tasks activate the existing sales_pipeline in the same transaction as
  the task, with immutable pipeline_history and a stable task-derived event ID.
  Retry does not create another task/history or reactivate a later intentional
  closure. Existing active requests remain unchanged. Notes and task completion
  do not activate/close work or update contact/CRM relationship status.
- Customer stages come from open Draft/Sent client_quotes, current Confirmed/
  Packing/Ready orders, or explicit reviewed manual stage choices. Multiple badges
  can coexist. Task-only active work has no invented business stage.
- Read-only /api/customer-workbench checks a revoked-token-aware Firebase identity
  and fresh admin/CRM-staff profile, then returns B2B communication dates only.
  It selects header metadata rather than message bodies, validates customer paths,
  paginates with a fail-closed bound and uses private/no-store responses. Customer,
  anonymous, service identity and staff without CRM capability are denied.
- Actual dates come from email/WhatsApp/Alibaba thread date ranges, recognized
  communication log dates, last_contacted and successful lastOutreachAt stamps.
  Draft/task/note/AI/import timestamps never count. Unknown/invalid source dates
  remain unknown/partial and cannot qualify for quiet-period targeting. This means
  latest *recorded* communication; unrecorded phone/personal WhatsApp activity is
  still the operator's responsibility to review.
- Dashboard links to Daily Drafts' explicit quiet90 mode. It filters existing B2B
  customers before the candidate cap, excludes leads and unknown/partial history,
  retains 14-day outreach and topic cooldowns, deduplication and manual blocks,
  and carries reviewed ongoing-work context. Failed history reads stop generation;
  there is no all-audience fallback or automatic draft/send on navigation.

### Schema and migration review, before any live write

No bulk migration/backfill, new collection, index or Firebase rule deployment is
needed or applied. Existing 20 reviewed positions/history and all task IDs remain.
The implementation reuses the already authorized manual pipeline transition and
its existing atomic audit-rule contract.

One optional internal customer field is added **only when an operator saves stage
choices**: dashboard_work = {stages: [exploration|quotation|order], revision,
event_id, updated_by, updatedAt}. Existing absent values remain absent. Writes
check customer existence, merge lock and original revision; same-intent retries
are idempotent. Clearing stages does not close other work. Existing customer
merge policy unions both manual stage sets and increments their revision, rather
than losing a source customer's project. Existing CRM-only parent rules protect
this metadata; it is not copied to portal accounts, catalogue records or exports.

Contact metadata is computed on read, not written as a new persistent tracker.
Review period is a browser preference separate from the saved summary period.
Future per-project records and per-customer "reviewed" tracking are separate work;
this slice does not automatically split historical free text into projects.

### Verification and lessons

Local checks: 151 CRM/workspace unit/integration cases; 122 AI/Daily Drafts cases;
13 pipeline/activation/stage/metadata emulator cases; 23 real Dashboard/CustomerDetail
sync cases plus 12 follow-up cases in each of three other timezones; 1,176 reported prebuild cases
and Vite build. Browser QA uses the real Dashboard/components and fictional
service boundaries, with ordinary isolated Chrome clicks and no customer sends.
Widths 1440/375/320 show no horizontal overflow; first task starts at 223/263/309 px.
It verifies B2B picker exclusion, dialog draft protection, one task save, work-stage
save, task completion preserving work, collapsed detail and full summary expansion.
Exact case totals/log paths should be rechecked with any subsequent change.

Ignored evidence: qa/out/dashboard-visual/ and /tmp/oc-dashboard-*.log. Browser
runner: qa/dashboard-workbench-browser.mjs (requires local Playwright/Chrome).
Permanent tests are included through test:customer-workspace and the existing
pipeline emulator/workspace-sync CI paths; quiet-mode cases run in test:ai-writer.

Lessons: keep contact review distinct from summary context and due dates; do not
interpret generic Retail industry tags as B2C; activate tasks with the audited
pipeline transaction rather than a second best-effort write; filter quiet customers
before the candidate limit; scope test selectors when adding a second select.
The old template-deletion test selected the new contact-review dropdown after
removing the topic dropdown; adding the topic select's accessible label fixed the
fixture while preserving coverage. No email was sent during tests.
