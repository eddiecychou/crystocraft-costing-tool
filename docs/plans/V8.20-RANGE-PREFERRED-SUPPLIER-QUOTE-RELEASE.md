fields. An open new-quote draft reuses its ID and uploaded files on retry.

## Hosted verification

- The designated QA admin signed in by email and password in a fresh
  Chrome context on each origin. The OC dashboard, Settings, Portal and
  Range loaded. The catalogue survived refresh, and the internal Range
  quote editor ran without a page or chunk error.
- The designated approved buyer signed in separately on each origin.
  The shop, Range catalogue and a product detail loaded. Refresh retained
  access. The Portal and internal Range quote routes redirected to the
  shop. No staff controls or internal quote fields appeared.
- Each hosted fixture chose one preferred quote with matching component
  costs, tiers, currencies and tooling. A stale note and PDF edit kept
  the newer preference, USD 1.222 approved cost and both source links.
  The added PDF bytes matched the fixture upload.
- A rejected new quote saved no business records and kept the open draft
  and its uploads. The next attempt committed once at 4.444, with matching
  preferred costing. After an intentionally lost confirmation, retry
  reopened the same quote ID with zero additional writes or uploads.
- Deleting the selected fictional quote cleared the active costing. The
  surviving quotes, original attachments and component stock stayed intact.
  The hosted cases used browser-only records and intercepted writes.
  The loopback Firestore suite separately verified competing transactions
  and permission-denied commits under the existing rules.
