# Portal approval decisions

Manual approval and claims of staff-issued invitations save the buyer account, customer link, customer-facing currency/rate/wholesale terms, private markup, invitation status and audit entry in one Firestore transaction. A staff member can approve from the account page with edited pricing or an internal test-account category in the same action. Fields outside that decision retain their saved values. Rejection keeps the existing pending account without granting access.

Each decision checks the current invitation and linked profile. When reviewers disagree, the first committed decision wins. The later reviewer sees a conflict and retains their unsaved form. A stale pending-account Save also checks the decision. Old records with conflicting account and invitation links require staff review. This release runs no data migration.

Approval starts the appropriate email after the decision commits. A Google buyer receives a Google sign-in confirmation and a password buyer receives a setup link. Staff can retry delivery in **Portal → Invitations**. If an attempt lost its delivery acknowledgement, refresh after ten minutes and check the provider before resending. The resend acts only on email delivery.

## Fictional checks

- The portal endpoint ran against the Firestore emulator. Two reviewers chose competing customers and prices, and separately raced approval against rejection. Readback checked the winning audit actor, account status, customer link, price, private markup, invitation and number of captured emails.
- A failure immediately before the transaction commit preserved all three original documents and sent no mail. Missing customers, mismatched account links, non-admin requests, a revoked invitation and a suspended buyer could not complete approval.
- Both email types were tested with delivery failure, concurrent resends and lost acknowledgement. A retry preserved a subsequent pricing correction. All test mail was intercepted.
- Two fictional admins used separate Chrome sessions on the same pending buyer. One submitted a markup of 2.25. The other saw a conflict, retained 3.75 in the editor and could not overwrite the saved 2.25 with Save changes. The account and invitation were both approved, and one fictional setup email was captured.
- The Google and password claim paths, interrupted registration, password setup and approved-buyer sign-in also passed in Chrome.
