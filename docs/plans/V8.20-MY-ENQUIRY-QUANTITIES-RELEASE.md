# My Enquiry quantities for the Crystocraft Range

Buyers choose pieces from available stock or whole cartons for made-to-order designs. A design can be requested both ways in one enquiry. The draft retains the unit, exact piece quantity, finish and colour across reloads. The confirmation and staff record store piece quantities for pricing and the carton count for manufacturing. Concept quantities express interest.

Existing enquiries require no migration. Older drafts with an ambiguous supply method keep their quantity and note until the buyer chooses pieces or confirms the displayed carton conversion. A missing carton size requires packing confirmation. A smaller stock balance prompts the buyer to revise the draft.

## Stock checks

The product page and enquiry use the same finish and colour limits. The authenticated submission function reads the buyer profile, visible Range variants, current carton sizes, prices, stock and reservations in a Firestore transaction. It combines demand on shared parts across lines and designs under the existing availability policy. Enquiries do not reserve inventory. Submitting the same saved request twice returns one enquiry. Direct buyer creation of top-level enquiry documents now goes through the checked service.

## Fictional verification

Two approved fictional buyers used Chrome with the Auth and Firestore emulators. Each added stock and made-to-order lines for the same SKU with a 24-piece carton. Their original piece quantities survived reload. Entering 99 with eight available stopped at eight. Two cartons showed 48 pieces.

Available stock then dropped to two immediately before Send. The server rejected the request, retained the draft and its note, and accepted the correction. The saved lines contained two stock pieces and two cartons, with a total of USD 100. A repeated submission resolved to the original enquiry. Separate cases covered demand shared between designs, partial cartons, ambiguous legacy drafts and mixed corporate/fabric enquiries. All email requests stayed local.

The permissions suite passed 113 checks. The local price, availability, draft privacy, image download and Firebase configuration regressions passed, along with the production build and undefined-name lint.
