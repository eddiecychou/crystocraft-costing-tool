# V8.20 — customer section spacing

Owner requested the spacing correction and then explicitly authorized deployment.
The shared customer accordion default changes from px-5 pb-5 -mt-1 to px-5 py-4:
16px top/bottom and 20px horizontal padding, without pulling content above the
header divider. Compose Message uses the same inset and removes its first
paragraph's negative margin. Custom table bodies keep their existing spacing.
No handlers, collapse keys, queries, permissions, fields or business logic change.

## Local verification

- Contacts, Company Details and Storefront Accounts on the supplied customer
  measure a 16px content gap below their dividers at desktop and 375px.
- At 375px, page and main widths are 375px, with no horizontal overflow in
  those sections. Contact links/address wrap normally.
- Compose Message and empty Contacts also measure a 16px gap; empty/populated
  sections still expand/collapse correctly.
- All 12 real CRM component cases and 93 workspace checks pass, with the full
  prebuild/build. Screenshots/measurements are ignored under
  qa/out/customer-spacing-release. No real sends or business-record edits.
