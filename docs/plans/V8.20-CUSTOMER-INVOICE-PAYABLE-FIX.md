## Problem and bounded correction

An app invoice can have line total USD 1,222 and approved Accounting Total
USD 1,200. `invoicePaymentTotals` already chooses that approved amount, but the
customer PDF and Excel only rendered Amount Payable inside the positive-deposit
branch. With an absent/zero deposit they showed only 1,222, and the PDF words
also used 1,222. Explicit approved accounting zero had the same problem.

`src/customer/CustomerInvoicePrint.jsx` now renders/exports Amount Payable when
it differs from the calculated total, independently of deposit visibility. With
no positive deposit the PDF amount-in-words uses `payment.invoiceTotal`; with a
positive deposit it continues to use outstanding. Deposit/outstanding rows stay
hidden for absent, null, blank or zero deposits. Existing line totals, precision,
currency, discount/charges and unadjusted output remain intact. The workbook
keeps its existing numeric-summary format; no new amount-in-words row is added.

The existing payment helper, admin invoice, proforma, ERP adapter, allocation,
registry, stored orders, calculation/validation policy and customer access rules
are unchanged. Customer output receives no internal adjustment reason, supplier
or cost fields. This is a display/export correction, not an approved-amount
change, cash receipt or historical repair.
