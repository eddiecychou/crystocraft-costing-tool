# V8.20 read-only Order Totals rows

ShipmentOrderTotalRows renders the computed subtotal, PI subtotal comparison,
charges and final total with its PI comparison. ShipmentForm computes and
formats the amounts, determines which comparisons to show, and renders the
editable discount, accounting and deposit controls.

Baseline af72605. The form fixtures compare the previous and extracted
rendering with fictional orders. Live QA edits a blank row and confirms
that the displayed currency and amounts follow the inputs and reset on
reload. Saved order verification uses read-only navigation.
