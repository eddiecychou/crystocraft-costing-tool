# Style Study — Gemini's own reading of the supplier house style

Generated by asking gemini-2.5-flash (text+vision) to describe the actual
illustration technique in the reference images, so the generation prompt
is built from the model's own reading of the style rather than a paraphrase.

Line work: Consistent medium-thin dark brown outlines. Outlines are present on all foreground and midground objects, defining their shapes. Background elements may have lighter or absent outlines.
Color: Muted yet colorful palette. Predominantly flat color fills. Objects typically use 1-2 base colors. Shading is minimal cel-shading, usually 1-2 shade steps per surface, or none. Shadows are a slightly darker tone of the base color or a muted grey-brown. No gradients or airbrushing.
Composition density: Highly dense composition, approximately 85-95% of the frame is filled with layered elements. Minimal empty background space, primarily limited to the sky.
Perspective: Stylized 3/4 isometric perspective. Objects and buildings are viewed from a slightly elevated angle, showing multiple sides, but without realistic vanishing points.
Scale/proportion logic: Stylized and exaggerated scale. Key landmark elements are often proportionally larger than real-world scale for graphic emphasis and recognition. Elements are not in strict real-world proportion to each other.
Texture: Simplified, patterned, and graphic textures. Foliage and cherry blossoms are rendered as stylized clusters of solid color with minimal internal detail or outlines. Brick and window patterns are indicated by simple, repetitive geometric shapes or grid lines. No organic or photorealistic textures.
Edge treatment: Hard silhouette overlaps. Elements are clearly layered with distinct, unblended outlines where they meet or overlap.
