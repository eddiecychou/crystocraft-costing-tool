STYLE AUTHORITY — La Salle Music Box (v1, from style-study.md, gemini-2.5-flash's own reading of the supplier reference images)

Do not rewrite this block per-call. Paste it verbatim into every generation prompt for this project. If the style needs to change, edit THIS file and bump its version — not the individual v-prompts.

Line work: Consistent medium-thin dark brown outlines on all foreground/midground objects; background elements may have lighter or absent outlines.
Color: Muted yet colorful palette, predominantly flat color fills, 1-2 base colors per object. Minimal cel-shading (1-2 shade steps or none), shadows as a slightly darker tone or muted grey-brown. NO gradients, NO airbrushing.
Composition density: HIGHLY DENSE — 85-95% of the frame filled with layered elements. Minimal empty space, only in small deliberate pockets. Do not leave large empty sky/background areas.
Perspective: Stylized 3/4 isometric — slightly elevated angle showing multiple building sides, no realistic vanishing point.
Scale/proportion: Stylized, exaggerated — key landmark elements larger than real-world scale for graphic emphasis, not strict real-world proportion.
Texture: Simplified, patterned, graphic — foliage as stylized solid-color clusters, windows/brick as simple repetitive geometric grids. NO photorealistic or organic texture.
Edge treatment: Hard silhouette overlaps, distinct unblended outlines where elements meet.

HARD RULE (do not name example logos/text in this block — same reason MAGISTER's style.js dropped "a goldfish is orange": naming a specific real-world text/logo invites the model to reproduce it elsewhere too): never depict any signage, lettering, badge, crest, or plaque anywhere in the frame unless a specific v-prompt explicitly asks for one in a specific reserved zone. If a reference photo shows real signage, treat that area of the building as a PLAIN architectural surface — do not mention the sign exists at all, do not describe it, do not ask to "leave it blank" (naming it still invites it) — simply omit any reference to that part of the source photo.
