# Crystocraft — Brand Identity Guidelines
*For design system creation*

---

## 1. Brand Essence

**Company:** Crystocraft — premium crystal and metal gift manufacturer
**Founded:** 1958, Hong Kong
**Signature line:** *Craftsmanship that catches the light — since 1958*

### Brand truth
Crystocraft masters both metal and crystal, in-house. The brand's entire visual language flows from one idea: **light caught by craftsmanship** — mirror-finish metal throwing light onto precision-cut crystal facets. Every design decision should serve that idea.

### Tone
Premium but warm. Precise but human. Heritage without being old-fashioned. The brand has been making things beautifully since 1958 and it shows — in restraint, in quality of finish, in confidence.

---

## 2. Three Divisions

One master brand, three audiences. The logo and typography never change. The **accent colour** is the sole visual cue that differentiates the three.

| Division | Full name | Audience | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gifts | **Crystocraft** | Gift buyers, collectors, wholesale | Bronze Gold `#996632` |
| Crystals | **Crystocraft Crystals** | Designers, jewellers, chandelier/lighting, fashion, interiors | Sapphire `#1C4F64` |
| Bespoke | **Crystocraft Bespoke Gifting** | Corporate buyers, marketing, procurement | Burgundy `#6E2433` |

---

## 3. Logo

### The mark
Trademarked butterfly mark + CRYSTOCRAFT wordmark (thin geometric caps with a crystal cross-hair "O"). Fixed — never redrawn, never modified.

### Versions
| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| Black on transparent | Light backgrounds, print, packaging, embossing |
| White on transparent | Dark backgrounds, header, dark section overlays |

### Division descriptor (Crystals and Bespoke only)
When a division descriptor is needed:
- Set in letter-spaced caps beneath the wordmark
- Separated by a thin hairline rule
- Cap-height ≈ 40% of the wordmark cap-height
- Always single-colour (black or white) — never the division accent colour
- Gifts carries no descriptor — the plain logo is the Gifts face

### Lockup rule
The logo is always **single-colour** — black or white. Accent colours live in the surrounding system, never inside the mark.

### Division accent colours
| Division | Accent | Hex | Light background | Hex |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crystocraft — Gifts | Bronze Gold | `#996632` | Beige | `#F7EEE3` |
| Crystocraft Crystals | Sapphire | `#1C4F64` | Sapphire Light | `#F0F5F8` |
| Crystocraft Bespoke Gifting | Burgundy | `#6E2433` | Burgundy Light | `#F5F0F1` |

### Extended palette (print and packaging)
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink Black | `#1C1C1A` | Deepest dark — foil base, packaging |
| Champagne Gold | `#C6A664` | Premium metallic finish — foil, engraving |
| Platinum | `#C9CBCC` | Secondary metallic — borders, fine details |

### Colour logic
The three division accents are all deep, muted jewel tones — Bronze Gold (topaz), Sapphire, Burgundy (garnet). They sit in the same register so the family feels cohesive. The accent is the only colour that changes between divisions — everything else is shared.

### Usage rules
- Logo is always monochrome — never coloured
- Sapphire: use sparingly in the Crystals context — restraint signals a serious manufacturer
- Champagne Gold and Platinum are shared metallic finishes across all divisions (print/packaging only)
- Dark sections: use Near-Black `#222222` or Ink Black `#1C1C1A` as the base

It appears as a kicker before section headlines. In Gifts context it's Bronze Gold; in Crystals context it's Sapphire; in Bespoke context it's Burgundy.

## 6. Visual Direction

### The one rule: light is the hero
Every image, illustration, and graphic should make *caught, refracted light* the subject. Sparkle off crystal facets. Glints off mirror-finish metal. Controlled and intentional, not decorative.

### Photography style
- Controlled studio and macro photography — no lifestyle or staged human scenes
- Dark or warm neutral backgrounds so light reads clearly
- Close enough to show craftsmanship — the facet edge, the join, the finish
- Real product photography always — never AI-generated product images

### Per-division visual feel
| Division | Photography | Background | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gifts | Warm, soft-directional light, gold catchlights | Beige `#F7EEE3` & warm neutrals | Precious, celebratory, warm |
| Crystals | Crisp, cool, edge light defining facets | White or near-black, clean negative space | Precise, technical, modern |
| Bespoke | Rich, controlled depth | Deeper tones, burgundy elements | Authoritative, premium, capable |

### Graphic style
- Restraint over decoration — white space is premium
- Thin lines, fine hairline rules — echoes the monoline logo mark
- No gradients, no drop shadows, no decorative flourishes
- Geometric, structured layouts — echoes the wordmark's construction
