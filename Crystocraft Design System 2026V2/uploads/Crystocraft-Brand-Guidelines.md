# Crystocraft — Brand Guidelines

*Craftsmanship that catches the light — since 1958*

A reference for the Crystocraft brand system: architecture, positioning, colour, typography, art direction, trust seals, and digital structure. Built to be handed to designers, developers, and suppliers as one source of truth.

---

## 1. Foundation

### Heritage
- **Founded 1958** as United Art (Hong Kong) by master metalsmith Mr. Chou — pioneer of the *cold stamping* technique that produces delicate metal gifts with an exquisite mirror finish.
- Global breakthrough came with the **Butterfly Suncatcher** collection — the origin of today's butterfly mark.
- **Crystocraft** was established in 2003 to carry the craft forward. *Use "since 1958" publicly; treat 2003 as internal/legal trademark history only.*
- Always express heritage as the **founding year (1958)**, never as a fixed number of years ("60 years") that goes stale.

### The brand truth
Crystocraft is the house that **masters both metal and crystal, in-house** — a combination no competitor owns. When Swarovski discontinued its crystal in 2020–21, Crystocraft built its own rather than compromise, mirroring how Mr. Chou refused to abandon cold stamping. The metal story and the crystal story share one arc: *quality kept alive when the rest of the market chased cost.*

### Crystal positioning
- Crystocraft manufactures **premium crystal components** to its own standard, controlled end-to-end.
- Proprietary **Dazzle Crystal Cut** — 32 facets, snowflake-inspired.
- **Sourcing flexibility** across the world's great crystal regions — Bohemia, China, Egypt — framed as *premium origins made to our standard*, **never as cost-driven**. The expertise lives with Crystocraft, not the supplier.

## 2. Brand Architecture — a Branded House

**One master brand: Crystocraft.** All trademarks, 20+ years of equity, and recognition stay consolidated under this single name. Three divisions are differentiated by **descriptor**, not by separate names.

| Division | Descriptor | Business | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flagship | **Crystocraft** *(no descriptor)* | Consumer giftware, wholesale & retail | Gift buyers, collectors, wholesalers |
| B | **Crystocraft Crystals** | Crystal components & crystal fabric — the premium Swarovski alternative | Designers, jewellers, chandelier/lighting makers, fashion, interiors, events |
| C | **Crystocraft Bespoke Gifting** | Custom corporate gifts, awards & keepsakes, end-to-end | Corporate buyers, marketing/events, procurement |

**Backstage (never market-facing):**
- **United Art** — the legal/holding entity and internal tooling (e.g. **UA-ProductManager** workflow app).
- **Dazzle Crystal Cut** and **Smooth-Spin** — product *technologies/features*, not sub-brands.

**The discipline:** one name unifies; descriptor, colour, photography, tone, and layout differentiate. A components buyer must never feel lost in a gift shop; a procurement officer must never feel they wandered into one.

## 3. Positioning & Taglines

### Unifying thread
> **Craftsmanship that catches the light.**

True literally (mirror-finish metal throwing light onto faceted crystal — the original suncatcher) and strategically (it leads with demonstrable craft, which is the brand's strength and its content engine). This is the master signature line, ideally paired with *since 1958*.

### Crystocraft — flagship (gifts)
**Positioning:** For people marking life's meaningful moments, Crystocraft creates exquisite metal-and-crystal keepsakes that turn an occasion into something treasured — drawing on a metalworking artistry perfected since 1958 and brilliant proprietary crystal.
**Tagline:** *Craftsmanship that catches the light* (also: *Gifts that catch the light*, *Made to be treasured*).

### Crystocraft Crystals — components & fabric
**Positioning:** For designers and makers who relied on Swarovski's discontinued crystal, Crystocraft Crystals supplies premium crystal components and crystal fabric matched to that standard — with the proprietary Dazzle Crystal Cut, flexible sourcing across the world's great crystal regions, better pricing, and the reliability of a manufacturer who makes them in-house.
**Tagline:** *Brilliance, crafted to standard* (also: *The brilliance lives on*).
**Note:** Keep "Swarovski" out of the tagline/lockup for trademark cleanliness; use "the premium Swarovski alternative" in page headlines and SEO copy. Descriptor signals premium; copy captures the search.

### Crystocraft Bespoke Gifting — corporate
**Positioning:** For brands and corporate teams who need distinctive gifts that reflect their identity, Crystocraft Bespoke Gifting designs and delivers fully custom crystal-and-metal gifts, awards and keepsakes — fast, at any scale, managed end-to-end — combining AI-accelerated design with a vertically integrated factory and craftsmanship since 1958.
**Tagline:** *From brief to brilliance* (also: *Your brand, beautifully crafted*).

*Light at the top, brilliance running underneath — one idea, three registers.*

## 4. Logo & Lockups

**The mark is fixed.** The trademarked butterfly mark and CRYSTOCRAFT wordmark (thin geometric caps with the crystal/cross-hair "O") are **not modified** under any circumstance. The visual identity is built *around* the fixed mark.

**Lockup rules:**
- Division descriptor is a **separate, clearly subordinate** element set in letter-spaced caps echoing the wordmark.
- Position: **beneath the wordmark**, separated by a **thin hairline rule**.
- Size: descriptor cap-height ≈ **40%** of the wordmark cap-height.
- The **flagship carries no descriptor** — it is the default face and wears the signature line *Craftsmanship that catches the light*.
- **Primary lockup is single-colour** (black, or reversed to white/ivory) so it survives engraving, embossing, foil, and one-colour print. Colour lives in the system *around* the lockup, never inside the mark.
- Maintain consistent clearspace around the full lockup.

## 5. Colour System

### Shared foundation — every division
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / heritage base | Ink black | `#1C1C1A` |
| Premium ground (prefer over stark white) | Ivory | `#F7F4EF` |
| Secondary text | Graphite | `#6E6C66` |
| Premium metallic (foil/engrave) | Gold | `#C6A664` |
| Premium metallic (foil/engrave) | Platinum | `#C9CBCC` |

### Division accents — the one cue that differs
| Division | Accent | Hex | Gemstone | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crystocraft (gifts) | Champagne gold | `#C6A664` | Topaz | Warm, celebratory |
| Crystocraft Crystals | Sapphire blue | `#1C4F64` | Sapphire | Cool, precise, modern |
| Crystocraft Bespoke Gifting | Deep burgundy | `#6E2433` | Garnet | Deep, authoritative |

**Logic:** Harmony comes from shared *depth and mutedness*, not matching temperature. All three sit in the same deep, slightly-dusty jewel register, so the trio reads as one family while remaining clearly distinct (warm gold / cool sapphire / deep burgundy span the wheel). The sapphire was chosen over green options because it reads precise and modern — right for a technical components audience — rather than traditional.

**Usage rules:**
- The **logo lockup stays monochrome**; accents appear only in full-colour web and print application.
- **Crystals: use sapphire sparingly** — lots of ink, ivory, and white space with macro photography. Restraint signals a serious manufacturer.
- Gold and platinum are shared metallic finishes available to any division.

## 6. Typography

Two-typeface system: a high-contrast serif for display, a geometric sans for everything else. Both are **free on Google Fonts** — a low-friction, near-zero-cost web addition. The **wordmark does not change**; this system is for the type *around* it.

| Role | Typeface | Alternate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / headlines | **Cormorant Garamond** | Fraunces *(warmer, sturdier at small sizes)* | High contrast literally catches light; heritage warmth; elegant contrast to the geometric wordmark |
| Text / UI / specs | **Outfit** | Inter *(more clinical for dense specs)* | Geometric, echoes the wordmark; clean and legible for body, navigation, technical content, proposals |

**Per-division emphasis (same two faces):**
- **Gifts** — serif-led, generous, editorial.
- **Crystals** — sans-led, **tabular figures** for aligned spec numbers; serif used sparingly.
- **Bespoke** — balanced, authoritative.

**Conventions:**
- **Eyebrow / kicker:** wide-tracked caps in Outfit, echoing the wordmark's letter-spacing and the seal type — used as section labels everywhere to tie back to the logo.
- **CJK roadmap (later):** for Chinese-language catalogues and the China market, pair with a CJK companion such as **Noto Serif / Noto Sans CJK**, chosen to harmonise with this Latin set. Does not affect current decisions.

## 7. Art Direction & Photography

### Unifying principle: **light is the hero**
Every image makes *caught, refracted light* the subject. Directional, controlled lighting that throws real sparkle off the facets and glints off the mirror-finish metal; tonal backgrounds (ink, ivory) so light reads; macro detail close enough to show the craftsmanship.

### Production strategy
- **Real photography for the product and the craft** — authenticity is the entire premise of a craft brand. The signature look is *controlled studio and macro craft photography*, fully producible in-house (no models or locations required). This aligns the brand's premium look with what the team is strongest at.
- **AI only for context** — scene backgrounds, corporate-context mockups, and especially **customisation previews inside Bespoke proposals** (show a client their logo on the piece before production). Keeps the craft real while letting workflow speed show up where it wins business.

### Per-division direction
| | Hero subject | Light | Ground | Styling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gifts** | The keepsake as a precious object | Warm, soft-directional, gold catchlights | Ivory & warm neutrals, soft fall-off | Minimal cues (ribbon, box); object-forward, **no staged human scenes** |
| **Crystals** | The cut itself — facets, refraction, fabric | Crisp, cool-neutral, edge light defines facets | Ink or white, clean negative space | Ordered grids, single macro hero, technical callouts (cut, colours, sizes) — *doubles as content engine & Swarovski-alternative proof* |
| **Bespoke** | Branded/custom pieces & the process behind them | Rich, controlled, premium depth | Deeper executive tones, burgundy in set | Brand-in-context; **brief → render → finished** sequence |

## 8. Trust Seals

Replaces the reassurance buyers lost when Swarovski withdrew its swan hangtag and certificate. The butterfly mark anchors each custom seal. **Monochrome by default** (survives engrave/emboss/foil/one-colour); accent only in print/web.

### a. Authentication seal — the hero
- Round seal: **GENUINE CRYSTOCRAFT CRYSTAL · DAZZLE CUT · 32 FACETS** around the butterfly mark.
- Stays entirely Crystocraft — the master brand *is* the mark of authenticity (no separate ingredient brand).
- Placement: finished-gift hangtags, component & fabric packaging, web badge on Crystals product pages.
- **Back it with a certificate of authenticity / spec sheet** for trade orders (material origin, 32-facet Dazzle Cut, quality standard) — the certificate is what closes quality-conscious specifiers. *The seal earns attention; the certificate earns the order.*

### b. Origin variant
- One seal whose **origin line swaps** ("BOHEMIAN CRYSTAL," etc.), used only where origin sells. Always paired with **MADE TO STANDARD** — keeps framing on premium regions and quality control, never cost.

### c. Heritage seal — cross-division
- **CRAFTED SINCE 1958 · CRYSTOCRAFT** roundel. Packaging, footers, certificates.

### d. Technology marks — product features
- **DAZZLE CRYSTAL CUT™ · 32 FACETS** · **SMOOTH-SPIN · PATENTED**. Compact badges flagging genuine proprietary advantages.

### e. Trade credential strip — B2B & corporate only
- Curated existing third-party marks: **Disney FAMA Audited · Sanrio Licensed · Shanghai Expo 2010 · Est. 1958**.
- Show only on Crystals trade pages, Bespoke proposals, and About — **never on a consumer gift box**.

**System rule:** exercise restraint — one authentication seal plus, at most, the heritage seal on any single item. Crowding a product with badges looks cheap.

## 9. Website & Digital Structure

### Core principles
- **One domain (`crystocraft.com`), subdirectories — never subdomains.** Keeps domain authority consolidated; also the lighter build.
- **Preserve existing URLs.** Reorganise through navigation and internal linking, not by moving pages. Move a page only if essential, and only with a one-to-one **301 redirect**. (Protects hard-won "Swarovski alternative" rankings.)
- **Homepage = a light "house"** establishing Crystocraft, 1958, and the signature line, then routing to three clear doors.
- **Themed hubs** apply each division's accent, art direction, and type emphasis beneath a **shared logo, header, and footer**.

### Structure
| Hub | URL | Key pages | SEO focus | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gifts | `crystocraft.com/` | Figurines, music boxes, drinkware, evil eye, roses | crystal gifts, figurines | Shop / wholesale enquiry |
| Crystals | `crystocraft.com/crystals` | Components, octagons, crystal fabric, colour range, authentication + certificate | Swarovski alternative, crystal components/fabric | Request quote / sample |
| Bespoke | `crystocraft.com/bespoke` | How it works, capabilities, case studies, customisation | custom / bespoke corporate gifts | Request a proposal |

### Content engine & workflow
- **`/blog` stays a shared engine.** Preserve existing URLs; tag posts by division; make high-traffic **Swarovski-alternative posts link directly to the Crystals enquiry** to shorten the path from traffic to revenue.
- **B2B enquiry forms are the front end of UA-ProductManager.** "Request a quote/sample" and "request a proposal" feed the workflow pipeline — a fast, polished response *is* the brand promise for Crystals and Bespoke.

### SEO rule of thumb
Subdirectories on one domain · preserve existing URLs · 301 one-to-one only if a page must move · internal links pass authority to enquiry pages.

### Phased rollout (light-touch)
- **Phase 1 (additive, no URL changes):** three-door navigation, accent theming, the two new hub landing pages, authentication seal + certificate on Crystals pages, blog-to-enquiry internal links.
- **Phase 2 (deeper craft):** reshoot hero imagery to the art direction; roll out the type system.
- **Phase 3 (optional reach):** richer UA-ProductManager front-end flows; CJK / China site.
