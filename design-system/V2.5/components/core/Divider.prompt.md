Horizontal rule with optional centred label. Used to separate content sections, between product details, and in form layouts.

```jsx
<Divider />
<Divider variant="gold" />
<Divider variant="subtle" />
<Divider variant="bold" />
<Divider variant="on-dark" />
<Divider label="Or" />
<Divider variant="gold" label="Collections" />
```

Use `variant="on-dark"` inside dark (ink) backgrounds. `variant="gold"` for decorative section breaks in the Gifts division. `label` is styled in the eyebrow/kicker style (tracked caps).
