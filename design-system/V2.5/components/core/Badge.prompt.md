Small uppercase label for status, categories, and division markers. Zero border-radius for major contexts; use alongside product titles and section headers.

```jsx
<Badge>Default</Badge>
<Badge variant="gifts">Gifts</Badge>
<Badge variant="crystals">Crystals</Badge>
<Badge variant="bespoke">Bespoke</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid-ink">Limited</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid-gold">Featured</Badge>
<Badge variant="platinum">Premium</Badge>
<Badge variant="new" size="xs">New</Badge>
```

Use `solid-ink` or `solid-gold` for high-emphasis status. Use division variants (`gifts`, `crystals`, `bespoke`) to mark cross-listed content. Keep badge text short — 1–3 words.
